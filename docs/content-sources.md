# Content sources for the Ahmed El Sayed platform

The current public domain is not serving the brand site at the time of review. It returns a hosting-provider default page, and the Internet Archive CDX query returned no archived HTML snapshots. The redesign therefore uses the owner's public social positioning and the repository brief, while avoiding claims that could not be verified.

## Verified positioning

- Instagram bio: Founder & Executive Director, freelance SaaS platform developer, digital marketer and funnel expert, and marketing evidence scraper.
- A public Facebook post on the page associated with `ahmedmokireldin` describes data lead packages for US and Canada, including company/contact attributes, and directs prospects to request a sample through WhatsApp.
- The redesign translates those public signals into four service pillars: marketing systems, data products, automation, and practical courses.

## Visual source

The homepage image `public/official-growth-map.jpg` is a local copy of the public image preview from the official Instagram post:

- https://www.instagram.com/ahmedmokireldin/p/DO_SGl6kYgz/
