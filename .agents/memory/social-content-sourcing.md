---
name: Social content sourcing
description: The platform's social links and video catalog must come from confirmed original assets.
---

Personal social URLs and video catalog data are content inputs, not safe placeholders.

**Why:** Reference sites for icons, fonts, stock profiles, or design inspiration do not identify the owner's real channels or establish permission to publish content.

**How to apply:** Keep the UI data-ready with explicit pending states until the owner supplies confirmed URLs, titles, thumbnails, categories, languages, and video links.
